# WCAG coverage

Generated by `npm run wcag:report` from W3C's official WCAG 2.2 data (https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/wcag.json, fetched 2026-10-25) and `src/wcag-map.mjs`. Do not edit by hand.

Level A and AA: **7 met by the framework**, **12 supported** (the framework gives the tool, the author applies it), **36 author responsibility**. Obsolete criteria (such as 4.1.1 Parsing) are left out.

Using this framework does not make a site conformant. Conformance depends on the whole page, its content and its behaviour.
